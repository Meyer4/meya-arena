Questions

Straight answers.

Is it safe? Why does Android show a warning?

Yes. Meya Arena requests internet access only — no contacts, SMS, location, camera or storage. Android shows a standard confirmation for any app installed outside the Play Store, including test builds from major studios. A Play Store listing is in progress for one-tap installs. Read the privacy policy.

Can I play with friends in other countries?

Today: battle skilled bots, duo queue with a partner bot, and climb the ranked ladder. Global real-player servers are the next milestone — the game, matchmaking design and ranked system are already built for it.

Will it lag on my phone?

The adaptive performance engine monitors your frame rate and retunes resolution, shadows and effects in real time. Leave Graphics on Auto and the game holds smooth frames from flagships to budget devices.

How much data does a match use?

Almost none. After the initial half-megabyte load, matches run entirely on your device and the game works offline.

Who made this?

An independent project — a playable gift that grew into a real game. George builds it; Mary inspires it.
